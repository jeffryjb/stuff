Rate, Unit Rate, or Neither?

Practice • Learn • Check Your Understanding

Rate

Compares two quantities with different units.
Example: 150 miles in 3 hours

Unit Rate

A rate with a 1 in the second quantity.
Example: 50 miles per hour

Neither

Does not compare two quantities with different units.
Example: 120 miles

💡 Tips for success
  • Look for two different units, such as miles and hours or dollars and items.
  • A unit rate has a 1 in the second quantity, such as “per 1 hour.”
  • If there is only one quantity, or the units are the same, choose Neither.
  • “Per,” “each,” and “for every” can signal a unit rate, but check the quantities.
