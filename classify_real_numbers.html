Classify the Real Numbers

Natural (ℕ)

1, 2, 3, …

Whole (W)

0 and natural numbers

Integer (ℤ)

Negative and positive whole numbers

Rational (ℚ)

Can be written as a fraction of integers

Irrational

Cannot be written as a ratio of integers

Real (ℝ)

All rational and irrational numbers

💡 Remember
  • Choose the smallest set that fits. 4 is Natural, even though it also belongs to larger sets.
  • In this activity, 0 is Whole, not Natural. (Some classes include 0 in Natural numbers.)
  • Terminating and repeating decimals are Rational.
  • Non-terminating, non-repeating decimals are Irrational.
